Hi all,

Like many of you, I've been worried about lifts getting stuck and alarms not working. I tried to put together something small that might help. It's a first attempt, so please bear with me.

A simple safety app for our building.

An example:
It's 11 pm and you're stuck in the lift.
1. Open the app and tap Johnson Lifts. It calls their breakdown line.
2. Tap Report, then Lift stuck, then your tower.
3. Tap Send. You get a ticket number, so the problem is on record.
4. If you like, one more tap shares it in the safety WhatsApp group.

You can also report smoke, a gas smell, loose wires, cracks or a blocked fire exit. Add a photo or voice note if you want. It works in Kannada, Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Bangla, Odia and more. All the emergency numbers are inside too.

Every report can be shared in our safety WhatsApp group, so we all see what's happening and can follow up together. Please join here:
